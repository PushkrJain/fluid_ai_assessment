Prepare a board-level proposal for an AI customer-support chatbot for a mid-sized e-commerce company. Our total budget is $20,000, we require 24/7 multilingual support and on-premises hosting, and we want to launch within four weeks. The teams involved, expected support volume, supported languages, current systems, and security requirements have not yet been decided. Analyze feasibility, state reasonable provisional assumptions, identify trade-offs, and recommend a realistic approach. Include an architecture overview, phased implementation plan, clearly labelled illustrative cost estimates, privacy and security risks, human escalation, success metrics, and decisions the board must make. Do not assume all requirements can be met within the budget and timeline; explain any material conflicts and remaining uncertainties.
